Our Story

A Rich Railway
Tradition Since 1989

The club was founded in autumn 1989 by World War Two Arctic convoy veterans Gilbert Hutchison and Archie Allan, who placed a newspaper advert. The first meeting at the Clyde Community Centre drew locals, Navy/MOD staff from Faslane, and electrical engineer Dave Forrest.

The club was formalised with Geoff Dalton (Chairman) and Mike Lovell (Secretary). Our logo is based on the North British Locomotive Company’s iconic roundel—nodding to Glasgow's proud engineering heritage.

We maintain a relaxed, skill-sharing atmosphere leveraging our members' diverse talents. Regular exhibitions at Modelrail Scotland, Paisley/Braehead, Summerlee Museum, Cathcart, and North Ayrshire. Page updated August 2026.

Timeline

Over three and a half decades of models, exhibitions, and community.

1989

Club Founded

WW2 Arctic convoy veterans Gilbert Hutchison and Archie Allan place a newspaper advert, founding the club. The first meeting is held at the Clyde Community Centre.

1991

First Exhibition “Trierro”

OO continuous/branch line debuts. Followed by the ambitious two-level OO loop “Seaforth.”

1995

Craigendoran Pier Debut

A magnificent 30×15 ft 1950s-themed layout with a painted Jeanie Deans paddle steamer and scenery by artist Neil MacLeod. Wins scenery & general cups at Modelrail. Retired to the skip in 2026.

2000s–2010s

Expanding the Fleet

King Street Gardens, Shieldinch (N), Levenford (N, American trains — 2014), and Clyde Street (1950s North Clyde suburban OO — 2016) join the club.

Nov 2018

Trenches Wins Tolworth Cup

The WWI supply lines layout wins the Tolworth Trainshow Cup, cementing the club's reputation for quality scenery and operation.

Feb 2027

Up Next—Model Rail Scotland

The club exhibits Millig Bay (N gauge) at the SECC, 26–28 February 2027. More to follow.
